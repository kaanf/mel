Edge cases

  • Payment succeeds but the app closes before credits show: credits appear on next open.
  • Teacher cancels a class with bookings: see requirement 2.2
 
# Requirements

## 1. Book a class

As a member, I want to book a class from the weekly schedule,
so that my spot is guaranteed.

- 1.1 WHEN a member books a class with free spots,
  THE APP SHALL reserve one spot, deduct one credit
  and show it under Upcoming.
- 1.2 WHEN a class is full, THE APP SHALL offer to join
  the waitlist instead.
- 1.3 WHEN a member cancels at least 12 hours before class,
  THE APP SHALL return the credit.

> [!ASSUMPTION]
> The waitlist is first come, first served. When a spot
> frees up, the first member on it is booked automatically
> and notified.

  the pack’s credits and email a receipt.
